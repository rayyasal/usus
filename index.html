Experience & Credentials

Before USUS, our founder led design and delivery work across some of the region’s most demanding retail and hospitality environments.

Our founder spent a year at a Beirut design consultancy — drafting for Ritz-Carlton, working on a signage project for Sofitel Morocco, and designing residential projects as a junior designer. She then spent three years in Dubai leading fit-out and main contracting, including the Chanel Perfumery Boutique in Dubai Mall, Virgin Megastore, Paul Cafe, Chez Charles, and The Butcher Shop & Grill for the Azadea Group.

Delivered as the youngest main contractor to complete a project of that scale for a global luxury brand — the Chanel Perfumery Boutique, the brand’s first standalone perfumery concept in the MENA region.

That grounding in real execution, under brands with zero tolerance for error, now shapes how our team manages every USUS project and turnkey partnership.

About USUS

USUS was founded on a simple belief: design means nothing without the execution to back it up. Our team’s experience spans from residential homes and boutique hotels in Lebanon to high-pressure retail and hospitality fit-out across the UAE — work that’s touched names like Chanel, Ritz-Carlton, and Sofitel. That range, from first sketch to final handover, is why USUS designs with intention and delivers on it, every time.
